This Privacy Policy explains how Snorry ("we", "us", "our") handles information
when you use the Snorry mobile application ("the App"). This policy should be
read together with our Terms of Use.


1. SUMMARY
----------

Snorry is a sleep snore monitoring app.

• Sleep audio, snore clips, session history, and waveforms stay on your iPhone.
  We do not upload them to our servers for storage or analysis.

• We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous app usage information
  (for example which screens you open and whether you started monitoring) to
  understand how the App is used and to improve it. Sleep audio and detailed
  health session content are not sent to Firebase through our integration.


2. DATA PROCESSED ON YOUR DEVICE (NOT UPLOADED BY US)
-----------------------------------------------------

When you use Snorry, the following is processed and stored locally on your
device:

• Microphone audio while monitoring is active, to detect snoring and related
  patterns
• Snore session records (times, events, settings snapshots)
• Short audio clips of snore events, where the App records them
• Waveform samples used for in-app charts
• Your alert and detection settings

This data is used only to provide App features (monitoring, history, in-app
analytics charts, alerts). We do not operate a cloud service that receives or
stores your sleep audio or full session recordings.

Deletion: You can delete saved sleep data in the App (Settings → Delete All
Sleep & Settings Logs). Uninstalling the App removes local App data from your
device subject to iOS behaviour.


3. DEVICE PERMISSIONS
---------------------

Microphone
  Snorry needs microphone access to listen for snoring during monitoring.
  Audio is analysed on your device. The system permission dialog explains why
  access is requested.

Notifications
  Snorry can send local notifications on your iPhone when snoring is detected
  (according to your settings). Notifications are delivered by Apple's
  notification system; they may appear on a paired Apple Watch like other iOS
  alerts. We do not need your email or phone number for this.


4. APP USAGE ANALYTICS (GOOGLE FIREBASE ANALYTICS)
--------------------------------------------------

We use Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited,
depending on region) to understand how the App is used and to improve stability
and features.

What may be collected (typically in aggregated or pseudonymous form):

• App opens and sessions (e.g. first open, session start)
• Custom events, such as:
  - Completion of onboarding (whether microphone and notification permissions
    were granted — yes/no only)
  - Start and stop of monitoring (whether push/sound alerts were enabled;
    duration grouped into coarse buckets such as under 30 minutes, 30 minutes
    to 2 hours, over 2 hours — not exact timestamps of your sleep)
  - Settings saved (which categories of settings changed, not exact values)
  - Tab navigation (e.g. Monitor, History, Analytics, Settings)
• Technical diagnostics that Firebase may collect as part of its SDK (e.g.
  device model, OS version, App version, crash-related data if enabled by the
  SDK)

What we do not send to Firebase Analytics through our integration:

• Sleep audio or snore recordings
• Snore clip file paths or content
• Individual snore event timelines tied to identifiable health profiles
• Your name, email, or account credentials (Snorry does not require an account)

Firebase may assign an app instance identifier on your device to distinguish
installations. We do not use Firebase for advertising personalization in
Snorry. Before collecting usage analytics, Snorry requests permission through
Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) system dialog. If you decline, Firebase
Analytics collection remains disabled in the App.

Legal basis (EEA/UK users): Legitimate interests in operating and improving the
App, where applicable; or consent where required by local law.

Retention: Google retains analytics data according to Google Analytics /
Firebase data retention settings configured in our Firebase project. Configure
and document your chosen retention period in the Firebase Console (e.g. 14
months).

More information:
  Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  Firebase privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

7. YOUR RIGHTS
--------------

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase,
restrict, or object to processing, and to data portability or complain to a

aksel.lindberg@lintech.no and we will help where we can.
